Overview

Independent Digital Finance (IDFI) is developing controlled digital market infrastructure designed around protocol-owned capital, isolated risk domains and verifiable operational controls.

Current development is focused on IDFI EX, a private market infrastructure layer in which public deposits, public liquidity participation and public trading are not currently enabled.

The system is designed to separate accounting capital from executable market capacity. Capital availability alone does not create trading authority. Market actions must remain within independently bounded capacity, inventory and risk controls.

IDOLL represents IDFI's first live on-chain market experience. Its existing PancakeSwap V3 market remains independently accessible on-chain, while protocol liquidity expansion and active market operations are currently paused.

Current Development Position

  • IDFI EX: private infrastructure development.
  • Public deposits: not enabled.
  • Public trading: not enabled.
  • Public liquidity participation: not enabled.
  • IDOLL protocol market operations: paused.
  • New smart-contract development: not part of the current development phase.

IDFI EX Architecture

IDFI EX is being developed as an isolated private market architecture. Its design principle is that capital, market capacity and execution authority remain separate control domains.

The infrastructure is designed to fail closed when required identity, accounting, capacity, inventory or isolation conditions are not satisfied.

Core Building Blocks

  • IDFI EX CAPITAL: protocol-controlled treasury capital reserved for future operational use.
  • Controlled Capital / CORE-HPP: protocol-owned capital admitted into the private operating capital domain under explicit accounting controls.
  • Safe Capacity & Risk Controls: bounded market capacity derived from available protocol capital, executable depth, inventory limits and operational risk limits.
  • Private Market Infrastructure: internal market logic that can produce bounded market-action candidates without independently authorizing real execution.
  • Asset-Local Inventory Domains: independent inventory limits for each supported asset without unrestricted cross-asset borrowing or balance substitution.

Controlled Capital Model

IDFI EX is designed around protocol-owned operating capital. Public liquidity providers, public deposits and unaffiliated third-party capital are not part of the current CORE-HPP model.

Capital Separation

  • Treasury capital remains separate from admitted operating capital.
  • Capital must pass explicit accounting and ownership controls before becoming eligible for operational capacity calculations.
  • Capital admission does not automatically create market or execution authority.
  • Public or unrelated external capital cannot silently enter the CORE-HPP accounting domain.

CORE-HPP Principle

CORE-HPP represents the protocol-controlled private operating capital domain used by IDFI EX. It is designed to remain isolated from public liquidity and unrelated third-party capital.

Safe Capacity & Risk Controls

IDFI EX does not treat available treasury capital as unrestricted executable capital. Market capacity is bounded by multiple independent controls.

Capacity Inputs

  • Available protocol operating capital.
  • Executable market depth.
  • Asset-local inventory limits.
  • Operational risk limits.
  • Price deviation and spread conditions.
  • Market-data freshness.

Decision Principle

A market-action candidate may only be produced when required controls are satisfied. Invalid, stale, excessive or incomplete conditions are designed to result in no action or denial rather than automatic execution.

Market Infrastructure

IDFI EX is currently being developed as private internal infrastructure. Public customer trading and public deposits are not currently enabled.

Market-Control Principles

  • Market actions must represent legitimate economic purpose.
  • Self-trading and synthetic-volume targeting are not permitted design objectives.
  • Candidate notional cannot be silently increased or substituted between control layers.
  • Asset identities and inventory domains must remain consistent through the control chain.
  • A market candidate does not independently authorize wallet, settlement or transaction execution.

Current Operational Status

IDFI EX remains in private infrastructure development. The current objective is to validate accounting, inventory, market-data and risk-control layers before any broader operational expansion is considered.

Security & Operational Controls

IDFI applies a layered security model across its current on-chain assets and the IDFI EX private market infrastructure. Security controls are designed to keep capital ownership, accounting state, market capacity and execution authority as separate control domains.

IDFI EX is currently private infrastructure. Public deposits, public trading and public liquidity participation are not enabled.

Current Access Model

  • Private infrastructure: IDFI EX is currently intended for controlled internal development and operational validation.
  • No public deposits: public customer funds are not currently accepted into the IDFI EX operating-capital domain.
  • No public trading: the current infrastructure does not provide public customer trading access.
  • No public liquidity admission: public liquidity providers and unrelated third-party capital are not part of the current CORE-HPP model.
  • Controlled authority: access to internal operational functions does not by itself create capital, market or execution authority.

Capital & Execution Security

  • Protocol-owned capital: the current CORE-HPP model is designed around protocol-controlled operating capital rather than public or unaffiliated third-party capital.
  • Capital separation: treasury capital remains distinct from capital admitted into the operating accounting domain.
  • Bounded capacity: available capital does not automatically become executable market capacity. Capacity remains subject to accounting, market depth, inventory and operational risk controls.
  • Asset-local inventory: supported assets are designed to operate within independent inventory domains without unrestricted cross-asset borrowing or silent balance substitution.
  • Execution separation: a market-action candidate does not independently authorize wallet, settlement or transaction execution.
  • No silent resizing: candidate notional and asset identity must remain consistent through applicable control layers.

Market-Control Security

  • Market actions must represent a legitimate economic purpose.
  • Self-trading and synthetic-volume targeting are not permitted design objectives.
  • Market capacity must remain within independently bounded risk and inventory limits.
  • Stale market data, excessive spread, excessive price deviation, insufficient executable depth or incomplete control state are designed to prevent a market-action candidate from receiving approval.
  • Invalid or incomplete control conditions are designed to fail closed rather than create execution authority.

Security Principles

  • Bounded Risk: market activity cannot exceed approved capacity and applicable inventory constraints.
  • Traceability: internal control decisions are designed to remain attributable and auditable.
  • Fail Closed: invalid, stale, inconsistent or incomplete control state does not create market or execution authority.

What is IDFI EX?

IDFI EX is the private market infrastructure currently under development by Independent Digital Finance. Its architecture separates capital ownership, accounting state, market capacity, inventory controls and execution authority into distinct control domains.

Is IDFI EX currently open to the public?

Does IDFI EX accept public liquidity providers?

What is IDFI EX CAPITAL?

IDFI EX CAPITAL refers to protocol-controlled treasury capital reserved for future operational use. Treasury capital remains separate from admitted operating capital and does not automatically become executable market capacity.

What is CORE-HPP?

CORE-HPP is the protocol-controlled private operating-capital domain used within the IDFI EX architecture. Capital entering this domain must remain subject to explicit accounting, ownership and isolation controls.

Does available capital automatically authorize trading?

No. Capital availability, market capacity and execution authority are separate control concepts within IDFI EX. Available capital alone does not create permission to perform a market action or transaction.

What is Safe Capacity?

Safe Capacity is the bounded market capacity derived from multiple controls, including available protocol operating capital, executable market depth, asset-local inventory limits, market conditions and operational risk limits.

A proposed market action cannot exceed the applicable capacity and inventory boundaries.

How does IDFI EX manage asset inventory?

Supported assets are designed to operate within independent asset-local inventory domains. The current architecture does not permit unrestricted cross-asset borrowing, silent balance substitution or unrelated inventory netting to bypass an asset's own limits.

Does IDFI EX target a fixed trading volume?

No. Fixed or synthetic volume targets are not design objectives. Market-action candidates must represent a legitimate economic purpose and remain within independently bounded capital, inventory, market-depth and risk constraints.

Does IDFI EX permit self-trading or synthetic volume?

No. Self-trading and synthetic-volume targeting are not permitted design objectives. Market controls are designed to preserve legitimate economic purpose and prevent control paths from being used to manufacture artificial activity.

What happens when market or risk conditions are invalid?

The architecture is designed to fail closed. Does a market-action candidate automatically execute a transaction?

No. Candidate generation and transaction execution are separate control stages. A market-action candidate does not independently authorize a wallet, settlement process or transaction execution.
